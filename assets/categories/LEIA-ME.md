## Prompt completo do álbum

> Use case: product-mockup. Asset type: single square category thumbnail for a Brazilian geek collectibles shop, matching straightforward white-background catalog photographs. Primary request: photorealistic studio product photo of one purple zippered trading-card collector album/binder, open in a three-quarter view, showing two black pocket pages each with a 3x3 grid of transparent card sleeves. Several sleeves contain colorful trading cards with yellow borders and tiny indistinct cartoon illustrations, while a few sleeves are empty to show their function. Show the purple cover/spine and zipper edge clearly so it reads immediately as a card storage album rather than a pile of cards. Entire album fits in frame, centered with small balanced margins and soft realistic contact shadow. Pure white background, sharp product details, clean retail photography consistent with supplied collection-category pictures. No people, hands, text, labels, slogans, watermark, price, or brand logos. No storefront mockup, no icons, no multiple panels. Output only this single album image.
